# Arcana Collections

The listener-facing collection selector replaces the old Summer/Autumn switch with four suits. The released 64-visual catalogue is divided evenly across them. The Winter/Pentacles development chapter now begins with a seventeenth Pentacles visual, **Punting with Ladies**. The old lineages remain in the art and in the historical [season development archive](SEASONS.md), but are no longer the way the player presents or filters the collection.

## Cups

Fluid, intimate, reflective and dreamlike scenes.

Swamp Bubbles; Lizard Louche; Goddess Kisses; Turtle River; Oil Slide; Oyster Pearls; Water Lilies; Inner Space; Champagne Fountain; Shy Smiles; Rat Race; Sunflower Smiles; Confessions of an Opium Eater; Life Studies; Dawn Cobweb; Lingerie.

## Swords

Conflict, danger, tension, strategy and difficult questions.

Arrow Storm; Knife Thunk; Lightning; Asteroids; War of the Roses; The Scream; Your Call Is Important to Us; Beowulf; The Life of Winston Churchill; The Iron Lady; Tony Blair: The Great Satan; P = NP?; There Will Be Bones; Spaced; Romans; Old School Games.

## Pentacles

 Craft, nature, work, place and material life.

Branch Hands; Tipu's Tiger; Climbing Garden; Bob Ross Garden; Cathedral Organism; Mondrian; A Load of Pollocks; Toadstools; Last Mow of the Year; Squirrel Sequestering; Autumn Drive; Harvest Time; Birds Eye; Plenty of Puff; Ralph's Last Watch; Hansel & Gretel; Punting with Ladies.

## Wands

Spectacle, motion, play, invention and unruly energy.

Fireworks; Pac Dance; Cephalopod Mind; Disco Jive; Glitter Fall; Butterfly Host; Octopus Occlusion; Mandelbrot Set; Eyes; Interzone Oracles; Hypnotic Flight; Kaleidoscope; Chimaera of Snake and Conga-line; Starry Night; Walk Like an Egyptian; Equaliser.

## Assignment Notes

This is a first-pass editorial grouping, not a claim that any visual has one true suit. Moving a visual between suits is a catalogue change; keep every visual assigned exactly once. The four suits contain sixteen released visuals apiece; the new Winter/Pentacles work is an additional seventeenth entry while that chapter develops. `app.js` contains the runtime catalogue because the offline Android player must not depend on fetching configuration from the network.

The existing Summer/Autumn source-family flag remains internal to select the appropriate renderer and Alchemy recipes. It is not a second collection selector and must not interrupt playback.
