# 32 Visualisations Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 6 July 2026

**Public URL:** https://andyjmyers.substack.com/p/32-visualisations-privacy-policy

32 Visualisations is a local music player and audio visualisation app for Android. It is designed to play audio files that you choose from your own device and to generate visualisations from that audio while it plays.

The short version is simple: **your music stays on your device.**

## What The App Does

32 Visualisations lets you select a folder of local audio files on your Android device. The app then plays those files and uses the audio signal on the device to drive visual effects.

The app does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not use analytics, and does not knowingly collect, sell, or share personal information.

## Local Audio Files

When you choose a folder or audio file, Android may grant 32 Visualisations access to the items you selected. The app uses that access to read and play your local audio files.

32 Visualisations does **not** upload your music library, copy it to a remote server, scan it for advertising purposes, or share it with third parties.

## Local Preferences And Session Data

The app may store lightweight information on your device so that it can behave consistently between sessions. This may include things such as:

- The selected visualisation.
- Playback and visualisation settings.
- Shuffle state.
- Recent playback position or session state.

This information is stored locally on your device for app functionality. It is not sent to us.

Depending on your Android device settings, the operating system may include some app data in device backup services. Those backup features are controlled by Android and your device or Google account settings, not by 32 Visualisations.

## Internet Access

32 Visualisations is intended to work offline once installed.

The Android app includes an embedded local web player. The app may declare Android internet access so that this packaged player and its local audio bridge can function correctly inside the app. 32 Visualisations does not use this to upload your music, track your listening, display ads, or send analytics.

## Wake Lock

The app may use Android wake-lock functionality to help keep playback and visualisations running while the player is active. This is used for playback behaviour, not for collecting personal data.

## Payments

If you buy 32 Visualisations through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. 32 Visualisations does not directly receive or store your payment card details.

Google may process purchase, account, tax, and transaction information under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

## Ads And Analytics

32 Visualisations does not contain third-party advertising SDKs.

32 Visualisations does not use analytics SDKs to track your behaviour.

## Data Sharing

32 Visualisations does not sell your personal data.

32 Visualisations does not share your music files or listening activity with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

32 Visualisations is not specifically directed at children. The app is intended as a general local music player and visualisation app.

## Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time if the app changes or if legal, store, or platform requirements change. The effective date at the top of the policy will be updated when meaningful changes are made.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, use the developer contact details shown on the Google Play store listing or the publisher's public software page at https://andyjmyers.substack.com/.
