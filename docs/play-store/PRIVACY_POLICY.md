# Privacy Policy for 32 Visualisations

Effective date: 5 July 2026

32 Visualisations is a local music player and audio visualisation app.

The app is designed to play audio files selected by you on your Android device. It does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not use analytics, and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information.

## Local Audio Access

When you choose a folder or audio files, Android may grant the app access to those selected items so it can play them. The app uses that access on your device to build and play your local playlist.

Your audio files stay on your device. 32 Visualisations does not upload your music library.

## Network Use

The app is intended to work offline. Its visual player is packaged inside the app and loaded locally.

## Data Retention

32 Visualisations may store lightweight local preferences, such as selected playback or visualisation settings, on your device. These settings are used only to make the app behave consistently between sessions.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, use the contact details provided on the app store listing or project page.
