## The Film

Space-Time Warp: The Blue Vortex, Beyond Jupiter, Event Horizon and Negative Eternity.

Wormhole: Prismatic Peristalsis, Liquid Polarisation, Velvet Singularity and Opalescent Inversion.

Opposing sliding panels and exploding titles bring the two worlds together. Measurements of the soundtrack's bass, midrange and treble drive the rendered motion.

Original music and art: **Andy J Myers**.

Code and creative collaboration: **Codex**.

Soundtrack: **Seattle Woman**, from the original recording, 00:24-01:24.
