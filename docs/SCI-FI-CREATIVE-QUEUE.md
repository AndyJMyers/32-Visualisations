# Science-fiction creative queue

Agreed on 8 November 2026. Preserve the order of the six main proposals, while balancing scale, inhabited places, machinery, encounters, action and abstraction. Each visual remains open to artistic review, not a production template.

1. Orbital: revised during review into immense shimmering tiled tubes against a flickering universe. Continuous slow morphs from torus to serpentine hose, interwoven knot and three-strand cable tangle. Four contrasting emerald, amethyst, opalescent and molten-metal treatments begin at different stages; broad musical light waves rather than frantic motion. Subtle breathing and travelling muscular contractions swell beneath the tiles, gently intensified by bass.
2. Docking Charges: immense spacecraft and a working station; bridge viewpoints and vast-machine motifs can enrich this.
3. The City Never Sleeps: rain, neon, flying traffic, elevated trains and moving advertisements.
4. First Contact: anticipation, emerging intelligence and music as communication.
5. Shields Up: coherent space-opera action with persistent consequences.
6. Matter Matters: transporters, holograms, force fields and programmable matter.

Additional inspirations supplied by the user: classic accelerating hyperspace; patient exploration of an alien planet; a spacecraft bridge; colossal industrial machinery; gravitational lensing; first contact; playful 1950s retro-future pulp; living alien intelligence.

Orbital's latest narrative: two living tiled orbitals trade alternating slaps, dismissive flicks and offended glances. Around 36 seconds the view begins withdrawing; by 51 seconds they are revealed as the eyes of a vast spaghetti-like intelligence. The 96-second staging cycle returns smoothly to their rivalry, while the geometry continues morphing independently. The fourth alchemy uses chrome reflections and brass seams.

Balance these against the main sequence rather than automatically making another tunnel or another battle. Hyperspace and gravitational lensing already have partial counterparts in Space-Time Warp, Wormhole and Event Horizon. Alien landscapes, retro-future pulp and living intelligence remain particularly distinct opportunities.
